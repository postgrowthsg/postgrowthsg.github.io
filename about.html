Postgrowth Singapore is a community exploring post-growth ideas and principles in Singapore: alternatives to an economy built on limitless growth.

This is our resource archive. It gathers the papers, books, articles, talks and communities that members and researchers have shared with us, sorted by theme rather than by date.

Best explored on a laptop. It works on phones, but the map has more room to breathe on a bigger screen.

Why it’s arranged like this

Most of what we share ends up in a chat or a feed, where the newest post quietly buries everything before it. We wanted the opposite: a place where things gather by what they’re about, so you can see what we’ve been thinking about, and where the gaps are.

So the archive is sorted by theme, not date. Nothing post-growth deals with stays in one box (care is also about work, housing is also about finance), so a resource can belong to two themes and sits between them. Themes with only a few items aren’t filed under “other”. They live in Still growing, at the snail’s tip, because that’s often where the newer ideas are.

It’s never finished. It grows as people share.

What we gather

To meet complex, systemic challenges, we need ways of knowing that can meet that complexity. So the archive holds many forms and levels of formality:

  • ResearchPublished journal articles, theses, academic reports
  • ArticleOpinion pieces, newspaper and magazine articles, essays
  • Community resourcePractical material made by a group or movement: guides, toolkits, databases, dashboards, campaign reports (e.g. a Wellbeing Economy Alliance guide)
  • Zine / own writingIndependently published work, including members’ own
  • BookFull-length books and book chapters, academic or popular
  • PodcastAudio series and single episodes: interviews, talks, discussions
  • VideoTalks, documentaries, interviews and explainers, long or short
  • PostSingle posts on social media or blogs, such as a LinkedIn post or an Instagram carousel

Resource or effort? If you can read or watch it, it’s a resource. If you can join, support or follow it, it’s an effort.

Effort: organisations, groups, campaigns and initiatives doing post-growth work, in Singapore and beyond. Switch the map to Efforts to see them, sorted by the PGS Collage themes: labour, extraction, production, markets and finance.

Reading the map

Each dot is one resource, and its shape shows what kind. The deeper the green, the more we’ve gathered on that specific theme. A dot sitting between two patches belongs to both. Tap a theme to browse what’s in it, and a dot to read about it.

Use the search box above the map to look for a word, a name or a topic. Typos are fine, and the first few letters of a word are enough. Use the switch beside it to move between Resources and Efforts.
